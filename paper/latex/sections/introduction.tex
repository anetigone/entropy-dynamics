\section{Introduction}\label{introduction}

Knowing which classifier states are unsafe does not determine how likely stochastic adaptation is to reach them. A complete risk function specifies the first object; an unlabeled score distribution determines the entropy dynamics. We isolate the second uncertainty by supplying identical complete risk information across environments and asking for an independent forecast before adaptation begins.

Entropy deterioration itself is established prior knowledge. POEM includes a one-dimensional Gaussian example of entropy collapse \cite{bar2024poem}; logit-adjusted TTA analyzes degradation under class imbalance \cite{weerasinghe2026}. Risk monitoring observes an evolving adapted model and test stream \cite{schirmer2025}, while action-identifiability work studies what unlabeled evidence determines about competing actions \cite{jhawar2026}. Here the action is fixed, risk is shared, and the target is a probability over a future independent stochastic path. The predictor is not asked to guess that path's realized branch.

Our contribution is the connection between this information experiment and a uniform approximation of actual entropy-SGD risk exit. It yields an exact consistency threshold, a finite-information minimax characterization, and a least-favorable direction that remains sufficient to describe worst-case difficulty in a larger bounded local class. Classical unstable-exit, experiment-comparison and efficiency tools are explicitly credited. The numerical contribution is a pair of valid decision bounds and a fixed-rule finite-batch comparison, rather than a claim that an optimizer found an exact least-favorable prior.

The paper proceeds from the observation experiment and common-risk construction to the exit approximation, statistical limits, unknown directions, numerical certificates and independent confirmation. Complete proofs remain in the linked chapters. Independent frozen-feature confirmation tests the prediction interface on unknown local visual environments. Earlier rebound, tolerance, source-transfer and stopping investigations remain supporting evidence.
